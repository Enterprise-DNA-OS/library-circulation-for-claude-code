# Selection and source notes

Checked 18 November 2026. Family: library-circulation, absent from the existing rebuild registry. Museum collections, knowledge bases and membership administration do not provide the physical lending and title-hold workflow authored here.

Scores out of five: annual bill 4, data-shaped 5, our buyers pay 1, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 4. Total 20/30. No Spydus buyer evidence found in the available customer brains or CRM snapshots. No live CRM was read. Search demand is qualitative, not measured volume.

The [Fremantle agenda of 13 July 2022](https://www.fremantle.wa.gov.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Agenda-FPOL-Committee-13-July-2022.pdf), pages 6-7, proposed A$60,000 excluding GST annually for Spydus licensing, maintenance and support. This supports a five-figure family bill historically. It is not a current invoice or a typical small-business price. The same record describes significant hardware integration and a past migration taking months, so this build makes no full-service switch-in-a-day promise.

[Civica's current product page](https://www.civica.com/en-gb/product-pages/spydus-integrated-library-management-solution/) describes circulation, reporting, public discovery, digital content and integrations. It gives no public subscription figure. The comparison page therefore uses words and no monetary figure. Spydus has configurable reporting; no claim is made that its dashboards cannot answer the ten questions implemented here.

Export support: [supplier description](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/203265447517068), [report CSV release note](https://cnrl.spydus.com/onlinehelp/2023R2_Reports.html), and [saved-query operations](https://cnrl.spydus.com/onlinehelp/Operations_menu.html). Search indexing exposed the help contents, but direct retrieval of those two help pages returned 403 during this run. Export capability is also described in the supplier listing. Exact CSV headers and status mappings need a real export sample. The included examples are synthetic.

Liberty was the second candidate: 1/5/1/2/4/3 = 16/30, with no verified annual bill. Club Caddie: 1/4/1/2/3/3 = 14/30, with no current vendor price and substantial point-of-sale scope. Neither was registered. Existing families and the charter's excluded industries were filtered before selection.
